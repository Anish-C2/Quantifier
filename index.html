7 · In real life

Where people hope to use this

Not for WhatsApp. Not for YouTube. For a few hard jobs.

Toy lock
Find a short number
Like a suitcase lock. The new hunt can check a pile of numbers with fewer tries. A long real password is still hard. This page will not open a real account.
Maze
Find a path
Same idea as a robot looking for a door. Or a van looking for a short road. Your car map today still uses a normal computer.
Medicine
New pills and batteries
Atoms are small and strange. This kind of math fits that world. People hope it helps make medicine. It is not a home app yet.
Plans
Shops, vans, time tables
“Which van goes first?” has many answers. A new computer may help pick a good plan. Not ready in your pocket.
Banks later
New locks on the internet
Some old online locks may get weak one day. People are already making new locks. That is the safe plan.
